# X Distribution System Glossary

This glossary defines the core concepts and terms used in the X Distribution System workspace.

## Terms

**X Distribution System**:
A local 14-agent intelligence pipeline for discovering, verifying, writing, grading, and tracking AI-focused content on X.

**Thunder**:
In-network amplification on X, where a post is distributed primarily to the author's existing followers.

**Phoenix**:
Out-of-network distribution on X, where a post is shown to non-followers, serving as the main organic growth vector.

**News Hunting Agents**:
A fleet of 7 specialized search sub-agents that scan Hugging Face, GitHub, arXiv, corporate blogs, startup funding, and macro feeds for relevant AI news.

**Reply Hunting Agents**:
A fleet of 7 specialized sub-agents designed to monitor and identify high-value reply opportunities on X based on credibility and relevance.
